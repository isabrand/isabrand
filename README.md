## Hello!

My name is Isabelle Brandicourt, and I just finished up my master's at the University of Washington working on underwater acoustic signal processing. Most of my work focuses on distributed acoustic sensing using fiber optic cables, marine mammal detection, localization, and tracking, and acoustic communications. Much of my work remains private until published, so free to reach out if you have any questions or interesting comments!
