## Hello!

My name is Isabelle Brandicourt, and I am a grad student at the University of Washington working on underwater acoustic signal processing. Most of my work focuses on distributed acoustic sensing using fiber optic cables. I focus on marine mammal detection, localization, and tracking via acoustic recorders based in Puget Sound. Much of my work remains private until published, so free to reach out if you have any questions or interesting comments!
